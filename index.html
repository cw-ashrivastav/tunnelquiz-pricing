Top-ups

Two prices. They never change and never expire.

Credits

₹15 each + GST

Any quantity from 50. Same price on every paid plan. Buy 200, pay ₹3,000. Purchased credits never expire and are spent after your monthly allowance.

AI credits

₹65 each + GST

Any quantity from 10, on Pro and Business. One AI credit writes about 150 questions or runs a handful of chat interviews. Never expires.

Credits carry forward

Unused plan credits roll over for three months while you're subscribed. If you cancel, your balance is frozen - not lost - and comes back the day you resubscribe.

Free credits reset

The Free plan's 50 credits arrive on the 1st and expire at month end. They can't be banked, and they aren't used while a paid plan is active.

Nothing charged on invite

A credit is spent only when a candidate actually starts a paper. Invitations, links and unstarted tests cost nothing.
Prices in INR before GST. Essential annual (₹14,990) auto-renews; Pro and Business annual are invoiced. One credit covers a whole sitting - a multi-section assessment costs one credit, however many papers it holds.
